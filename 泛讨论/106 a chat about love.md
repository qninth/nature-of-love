# 106 a chat about love

> Posted by 花生和尚 on 二 27th, 2012 in 杂论

Love exists, like a rock, not like smoke. It’s not a tool to mine happiness.

**谈话者A：** So it does not necessarily lead to happiness?

No. It leads to bond, unbreakable bonding. This is the result. Others are just its effects. If you seek happiness only, you shall not consider love as an option. What I speak in the group is not praising love and recommending it. I’m acctually warning. BE CAREFUL OF WHAT YOU ARE WISHING FOR. YOU SHOULD KNOW IT BEFORE YOU PURSUE IT.

**谈话者A：** Do people have an essential need to love?

Yes. It’s in their souls. That’s how they are made. That’s why human has this amazing thing called “society”. When the first ape encountered another ape who is not a family member, he gave him help instead of fist.  That’s how love begins. It’s irrational.  But it works amazingly. Those apes overpowered the ones who didn’t do things this way.

**谈话者A：** So that is how it started……

Yes. It’s simple math. Those who love have a better fighting chance. The symbolic behavior of love is to raise someone else’ baby, which is very common in many spicies. But to die for others out of no reason is a human thing. We die for strangers who is not our family at all. Soldiers risk their lives for reasons no animal can imagine. What a madness is that?! Think about it. Is there any rational utilism reason to do that?

**谈话者A：** No.

Under what term a man SHALL risk his life to charge machine guns? Or become a spy who has no hope of returning home? You can see that countries lacking these people are weak, meant to be destroyed. They just can’t survive. It has to be motivation beyond reason so that reasons can’t fight it. If reason can fight it, reason might win. Then the society is doomed.

**谈话者A：** So it has to be this way for the society to exist?

Yes. No one will love for reasons because it can’t work. If love is based on reason, it can’t fight reason itself, or else there will be a huge crisis, fatal crisis, because other societies can do things you can’t and cost a lot less. (This not only applies to) society, it might just be a company, or a troop, or even smaller unit.

**谈话者A：** Could be any group.

Yes. Love made human human, not vice versa.

**谈话者A：** So we have to love. Can one live without love?

No. I mean, you can resist love. It’s only a strong and essential urge. You can fight it if you’d like to. You can lose this cause if you are proper “trained“ .  Some find it very benificial to cut off love.

**谈话者A：** Trained?

Yes. Like most Chinese young ones are raised. They are trained not to love.

**谈话者A：** How?

They are taught not to trust, not to believe in anything. They are taught that the very idea of “believe in“ is a form of stupidity. “How can you believe without proper reasons“ , they are told.

**谈话者A：** No trust. Is that it?

No. Lack of capability to believe in anything. Their freedom is barred by evidence. They can’t go any further. They just can’t. When it comes to doing things out of love, they are afraid that there’s always a lack of evidence to prove that they are absolutely loved.

**谈话者A：** What evidence?

Evidence to help them believe. Do you know why those who refuse (to believe in) god is most likely cursed？

**谈话者A：** What do you mean by cursed?

Because among all those beliefs, God is the easiest one. It cannot be proven wrong with any logical argument. Believing in god will cost you nothing but a tiniest leap of faith. If you think it is unthinkable, what else can you believe in? You may refuse to believe in god, but you may not refuse the ability to believe. To believe in god is merely a “test run” .

**谈话者A：** It is definitely not unthinkable, but not everyone will go for it.

Yes. Stopping at “not unthinkable“ is okay. Go further to “unthinkable”, you are pretty much casting curse on yourself. Where have you planned for love to stand then? Love is far more difficult to believe in than the concept of an all mighty god. Sometimes there is no evidence at all and no matter how hard you dig, there is no such thing called “enough evidence for love“. It simply doesn’t exist. If you try to find reasons to love, you will fail too. Isn’t that a curse?

**谈话者A：** Not everything is rational.

It’s rational at a higher level, and there IS a higher level. “Believing in” is rational in a way that reason cannot reach.

**谈话者A：** How can we talk about rationality without reason though.

A problem that can be solved by reasoning is not a problem at all. It’s just a puzzle. The real problem is the one that cannot be weighted, cannot be broken down, cannot be avoided, and cannot be transformed.

**谈话者A：** So how can we solve the problems without reasoning? Do they have an answer?

Turn to beliefs. Turn to courage.

**谈话者A：** Turn to faith?

Faith is the courage of your beliefs. Courage is the very nature of faith. In fact, the word re-li-gion    means “bunch of beliefs“. Gion means bunch of things. Rely…..you know what that is. Religion is not a group of people, but group of beliefs. It’s not an organization of persons. It’s a logical constructure.

**谈话者A：** It is often perceived as the people though, the believers.

If you look into it carefully, you will find that at many times it’s not referring to the people. When it comes to that, they will use other words like CHURCH. Take it as beliefs, then the sentence will often make more sense.

When someone says “my religion“ is he talking about his fellow believers or his beliefs? It’s very obvious after that is pointed out. And the word “religiously“ …It doesn’t make sense at all if you think religion as a group of people. In fact the Chinese term zongjiao is not religion, but “religious organization”. People just use it without proper caution and it caused all kinds of misunderstandings.

**谈话者A：** Yeah that could be misleading.

It will cost our fellow Chinese. Sigh.

**谈话者A：** Who translated it……?

It seems to be Japanese.

(So they are screwed, too?)

**谈话者A：** That is too bad……

No. It’s our own bad that we use our language so carelessly. Most of us just don’t have what it takes to speak accurately. Chinese can be as accurate as needed as long as the user be careful as well.
